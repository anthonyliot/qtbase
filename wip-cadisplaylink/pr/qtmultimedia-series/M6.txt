darwin: Poll for video frames in sync with the display the video is on

AVFDisplayLink paces how often the AVFoundation media player polls for
a new decoded frame. On macOS it used the display link of the main
screen, the screen of the window with keyboard focus when it's created,
not necessarily the one the video is shown on, so the video could be
paced by another, slower display.

Let video outputs tell their platform video sink the window they show
the frames in, with a new QPlatformVideoSink::setDisplayWindow(), which
QVideoWindow (and so QVideoWidget) and the QML VideoOutput call. Unlike
setWinId(), it doesn't make the sink render into the window. The
VideoOutput passes its window as is: a QQuickWidget keeps the screen of
the window it renders to offscreen in sync with its own.

On macOS, the darwin sink then uses the display link of the window's
screen, found through the QNativeInterface::QCocoaScreen of the
window's QScreen, and recreates it when the window moves to another
screen. Like the main screen's display link before, it keeps delivering
while the window is hidden, as the frames may be used for more than
showing them. Without a window, or on other platforms than cocoa (e.g.
offscreen), it uses the main screen as before. iOS is unchanged.
