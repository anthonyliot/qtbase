Report the stream frame rate of played video frames

The FFmpeg and AVFoundation media player backends know the frame rate
of the video stream, but left it at 0 in the format of the frames they
deliver, so a video output couldn't tell the rate the content plays
at.

Set it from the average frame rate of the stream with FFmpeg, and from
the nominal frame rate of the video track with AVFoundation, which was
already used to estimate the end time of each frame. The other backends
still report 0.
